# Sound

File: `src/audio.js` (all of it). Called as `audio.sfx(name, {pan, vol})`; unknown names are silently
ignored, so check a new name exists in the effects list.

- Procedural Web Audio music + ambience per room (and per zone in the stages), crossfading on change
  (`setScene(room, zoneName)`); none in the Music House; houses reuse the tutoring room's music box; the harbour has a sea waltz with waves and gulls; the east cliffs a slow, airy tune with wind, seabirds and the sea booming below; the ferry's deck (both crossing rooms) a rolling 6/8 shanty with the paddle wheel churning and the ship's bell; Icefang Hamlet (`fjord`) a slow minor waltz on a fiddle-like line over a drone, with far bells and the lamp-fire crackling; Willowmere (`willowmere`, a lazy riverside tune), Saltspray Coves (`coves`, breezy shore strum), the Tidemother's den (`tidemother`, a heaving 6/8), the Mycelium Deep (`deep`, a hushed music box with drips), Glowroot Hollow (`glowroot`, racing glockenspiel over the heart knot's beat), the Knotmole's burrow (`knotmole`, a stubborn 6/8 with digging thumps), the Old Oak Canopy (`canopy`, a sunny swaying 3/4 with birdsong, a woodblock and a far-off woodpecker); Wreck Cave (`wreck`, a slow 3/4 shanty on a squeezebox with drips, a creaking hull and a far-off bosun's whistle), the Thornheart (`thornheart`, a stern E phrygian tune over a slow heartbeat, harp notes falling like petals), Keeper's Spine (`spine`, A dorian, a horn over open fifths, the wind, a far bell and a kite's cry) and the Root Warden's court (`warden`, stone footfalls, low brass and a bell tolling every bar); the Rimeglass Glacier (`glacier`, B minor, a bowed pad and a glockenspiel like light through ice, the ice groaning far down and a rising shimmer for the aurora); F2's Whale Skerries (`skerries`, a slow A dorian 3/4 fjord song with waves on the shingle, Hakon's wobbly bone flute and a far whale call), the Narwhal Grotto (`grotto`, glassy and hushed, drips and the pod's old call) and the Old Narwhal's den (`narwhal`, heavy and sad, her gliding song over a pounding pulse); F3's Aurora Heights (`heights`, very slow, raised-4th dorian, swelling pads, star bells and a high aurora whistle) and the Blue Halls (`bluehalls`, F# minor, a low drone, the glacier creaking and a far-off chisel); FB1 (b)'s Sunken Road (`sunkenroad`, the Thornheart's tune an octave down over a drone, the heartbeat closer, drips and creaking roots) and the Road's End (`roadsend`: no tune, a held note and heartbeats out of time with each other, more, louder and slower the deeper you are: `audio.setDepth`, from `roadsDepth` in `src/roadsend.js`); FB2 (b)'s Old Ice (`oldice`: the Blue Halls' music a fifth down and slower, the bed creaking, a chain clinking once far off) and the Long Stair (`longstair`: the tune thinning out by the middle into the ice's hum, two low notes beating, a slow breath, a chain clinking nearer and more often the deeper you are: `audio.setDepth` from `stairDepth` in `src/longstair.js`; `chainClink`); FB1 piece 3's Ladon's den (`ladon`: every one of those hearts at once on its own period under a low E phrygian drone, a creeping bowed line and creaking canes; its intensity is 0.7 in a final boss's last phase and 1 once it enrages, set in `src/rpg.js`, quickening the hearts, adding a war drum and low brass, then doubling every heartbeat and dragging the drone sharp); FB2 (c2)'s Wolf's Hold (`fenrir`, slot 52: F# minor, slow, the Long Stair's two beating notes an octave up as its hum, a glassy pad, footfall drums and a wolf's howl for a lead, each note sliding up into its pitch; it layers in by phase, since `src/rpg.js` gives a final boss's earlier phases `PHASE_INTENSITY` a step: chains and a walking bass in phase 2, its sons answering the howl and a gallop in phase 3, pounding drums, low brass and the howl doubled in phase 4, and the enrage puts the light out: the pad drops away and a semitone grinds against the hum); F4's Queen's Bridge (`qbridge`, a slow B minor 3/4, wind in the arches, a walking bass, a guard's horn call and a far bell) and Sigrun's den (`sigrun`, a B harmonic minor march, her footfalls and spear, the oath on a horn); atlas Phase 5's Forgehold (`forgehold`, a slow D minor dirge on a low reedy horn over a drone, the great forge's anvil ringing two strikes a bar, the bellows and the island's rumble) and the Lodestar's deck (`straits`, the ferry's shanty slowed into minor on a fiddle-like line over a fjord drone, the hull creaking, a hiss of steam and far geysers); new mobs borrow legacy mob voices; boss tracks in dens, intensified in a boss's last
  stage (`setIntensity`).
- Effects for slaps, swings, hits, every skill, boss attacks, coming out of the Rebirth Pool (`reborn`: a splash, bubbles, a rising shimmer, a warm chord; quieter and panned for someone else), per-mob hurt/death voices
  (`sfx('mobHurt', {kind})`), the shop, scrolls, level up, doors, splats.
- Esc menu → "Music: on/off" (localStorage `slimeville-music`). `audio.unlock()` runs on every gesture.
- The overall music level is `MUSIC` at the top of the file, kept low on purpose. Nobody has listened to
  the audio yet: levels were measured offline (`createEngine(ctx)` renders into an OfflineAudioContext),
  so adjust on player feedback.
