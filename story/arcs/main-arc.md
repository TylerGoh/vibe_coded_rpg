# Main arc: The Drift

Four acts, one per region, and an open ending. Each act ends with a boss that hands over **proof**,
which opens the next tier of truth with the NPCs. Progress is per player, but the world is shared, so
the story never needs one player's actions to change the world for everyone (except the finale,
which is a deliberate group event, see below).

## Prologue: the restless wood (Lv 1–3)
The Wildwood's creatures are acting up. Elder Mochi, sleepily: "the old tree's leaves are turning early
this year, hmm…". A Keeper stone has surfaced in Sprout Meadow overnight. Clio wants a rubbing of it.

## Act I: Greenhollow (Lv 1–12): "Something under the island"
- **Sprout King**: comic opener. He drops a glowing sliver; Mochi goes quiet when he sees it.
- **Mother Mushroom**: when freed, the mycelium carries *voices from across the sea*. Other islands are
  real and close enough to hear, just.
- **Bramble Behemoth**: the briar opens onto the Keeper ruins and the carving of a great wheel.
- **Act turn:** from the east cliffs players see the open sea for the first time. Captain Brine agrees
  to run the ferry north, "while the crossing's still short enough".

## Act II: Frostpeak (Lv 40–100; the legacy ridge and summit 40–60 since the rebalance): "The world was one"
- The first crossing (the ferry room). The sea is grey and the aurora grows overhead.
- Icefang Fjord: the fjord folk, Old Skaldi's songs about Pangelea and the Stopped Hour.
- **Frost Queen Glacia**: she held on until her people froze. Letting go of the ice bridge is the first
  grown-up beat in the game.
- **Astral Wyrm**: the stars go back to their places. Ships can navigate again.
- **Act turn:** Skaldi: "The sea is wider than it was in my mother's time. It is wider than it was last
  winter." The drift is speeding up.

## Act III: Emberdeep (about Lv 90–150; the legacy stage 90–115 since the rebalance): "Who broke the world"
- The long voyage through the Steam Straits.
- The forge-slimes' anger, the giant undelivered gears, the memorial wall.
- **Ignis**: the truth about the Tidewheel, the Keepers, and the tooth that snapped.
- **Pyra**: the cycle is stuck; she can't finish dying or being reborn. She shows the way up.
- **Act turn:** back home, Mochi finally tells the whole story: he was Pell's apprentice. "Bring him
  home if you can. He was kind, once."

## Act IV: the Aether Citadel (about Lv 140–200; the legacy stage 140–170 since the rebalance): "The Stopped Hour"
- The balloon climb. The whole archipelago, small, below.
- The Skydocks and Bastion: automatons still keeping a dead city. Keeper echoes replaying their last hour.
- **The Clockwork Colossus**: obedience without malice.
- **Chronos**: *Time Stop*, *Collapse of Hours*. Beaten, he's freed from the hour and is Pell again.

## Finale: restarting the Wheel `[proposed]`
A **server-wide event**: the Wheel needs many slimes at once (a shared goal, like the floor piano or
the votes, which fits a game about friends). When it turns: every sky changes, the Maelstrom calms, the
islands stop speeding apart, Pyra is reborn properly, and Pell comes to the apple tree. It happens once
for the world and is remembered forever (in the museum's Slime Ages wing, with everyone's names).

## After: the open sea
The routes stay open. The next age's stories happen across water: lost islands, the Sunken Reach, the
other Keepers, the first stirrings of the next Gathering. See `../expansion.md`.
