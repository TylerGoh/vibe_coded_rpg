# Canon

The settled facts. Short on purpose: AI characters get fed this text (sliced by tier, see
`delivery.md`), and anything here they'll repeat as truth. Change it only with the owner's okay.

Status: **reviewed by the owner (2026-10-24).**

## Tier 0: what everyone in Slimeville knows
- Slimeville sits on Greenhollow Bay, on the island of **Greenhollow**, in the **Glass Sea**.
- The big apple tree in the village is very, very old. Elder Mochi has looked after it for as long as
  anyone remembers.
- East of the village the land rises into **the Wildwood**: meadow, mushroom lowlands, then the briar.
- There are other islands far across the sea, more than anyone has counted. Few living slimes have
  visited one. Sailors' songs say the sea gets a little wider every year.

## Tier 1: learned in the Wildwood
- Old stones in the Wildwood carry the marks of the **Keepers**, an order from long ago.
- The mushroom network under the Wildwood reaches under the sea. Mother Mushroom heard the other
  islands through it before she fell ill.
- Each great beast of the wilds carries a glowing shard in its body. These are **Anchor shards**.

## Tier 2: learned in Frostpeak
- The world was once one land, **Pangelea**. It broke apart in **the Stopped Hour**.
- The islands are drifting apart, and the drift is getting faster.
- The stars fell out of place when Pangelea broke. Since the Astral Wyrm fell, ships can't steer by them.
- Frostpeak's queen froze the strait to hold her island close. The ice is cracking.

## Tier 3: learned in Emberdeep
- Pangelea breathed: it gathered together and drifted apart over ages, turned by the **Tidewheel**, a
  clockwork that the Keepers tended. The Drift was never meant to be forever.
- The forge-slimes of Emberdeep built the Tidewheel's gears. Ignis was their master smith.
- The Keeper of Hours tried to stop the Wheel to prevent the Drift. It jammed, so the land breaks apart
  and never returns.
- Pyra marks each turn of the cycle by dying and being reborn. With the Wheel jammed she can't finish.

## Tier 4: learned in the Citadel (endgame)
- The Aether Citadel was the Keepers' seat at the heart of Pangelea. It tore loose into the sky in the
  Stopped Hour.
- The Clockwork Colossus is the Tidewheel's guardian body.
- The Keeper of Hours is **Chronos**. Before, he was called **Pell**. He means to stop time entirely,
  freezing every island before it drifts out of reach.
- Elder Mochi was Pell's apprentice. The apple tree grew from a cutting of the **Heartroot**, the root
  that once ran under all of Pangelea.

## Deliberately open (don't close these in canon)
- How many islands Pangelea broke into. The charts know only a handful, and more drift into reach.
- How many Anchor shards exist. The Tidewheel had many teeth, and the bosses carry only some of them.
- Who the other Keepers were and where they went.
- What lies under the Glass Sea, and what is out past the edge of the charts.

## Rules of the world
- Slimes are jelly. They don't drown easily, but deep cold sets them solid and great heat thins them out.
- Gold, potions, levels, skills and fainting are real in-world things. No fourth walls are broken.
- Real players are villagers of Slimeville (the `VILLAGERS` list in `server/characters.js`).
