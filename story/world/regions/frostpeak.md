# Frostpeak (far north)

**Tone:** quiet, cold and melancholy. Fewer jokes. People here are stoic and talk in short sentences.
The first real sadness in the game.

## Getting there
The ferry from Greenhollow Harbour, through a grey sea with icebergs, and the aurora growing overhead
(exists: `docs/places/crossings.md`). She ties up at Icefang Hamlet's quay. (Until atlas Phase 4 the fjord
was frozen and she used a rough jetty under the ridge; the ice went out with that update.)

## Landscape, shore to summit
1. **Icefang Fjord** (exists, a safe town room: `docs/places/fjord.md`): the landing. A narrow fjord of
   black rock and blue ice, and a fishing hamlet of turf-roofed huts round a lamp-fire. A great **ice
   bridge** that Queen Glacia froze across the strait to the next island hangs in the sky, cracked down the
   middle. The **Lodestar**, the old ship that sailed south, is laid up at the quay: no stars to steer by.
2. **Frostpine Ridge** (exists): snow forest on the climb. Frostlings, yeti cubs.
3. **Starfall Summit** (exists): a crater where the **Astral Wyrm** fell. Glassy ground and crystal golems
   grown from star-dust. The sky up here is full of stars that are visibly *in the wrong places*: some
   are smeared and some are clumped.

## People
**The fjord folk:** pale, frosted slimes who go half-solid in the cold and move slowly. They remember the
Queen as good. AI characters (exist): **Old Skaldi**, a lamplighter who keeps the hamlet's fire and the
old songs (which hold Tier 2 lore); **Netmender Runa**, the trader; **Tove**, his granddaughter, who
feeds the yeti cubs. Mentioned only: Ylva (Skaldi's late wife), Bjarn (Runa's husband, a fisher), Arne
(Runa's cousin, the Lodestar's skipper).

## Story role
Act II. The world is bigger and sadder than you knew. Glacia's frozen bridge is a small version of
Chronos's mistake: she held on so hard that it froze her people. Beating the Astral Wyrm puts the stars
back, so ships can steer again, which opens the voyage south.

## Change from today
The trail from the Wildwood is gone: Frostpeak is reached by the ferry, which lands at the hamlet (Phase
4). It's still left by a "Caldera pass" at the summit, which goes when the Steam Straits ship runs.
