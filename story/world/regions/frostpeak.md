# Frostpeak (far north)

**Tone:** quiet, cold and melancholy. Fewer jokes. People here are stoic and talk in short sentences.
The first real sadness in the game.

## Getting there
The ferry from Greenhollow Harbour, through a grey sea with icebergs, and the aurora growing overhead
(exists: `docs/places/crossings.md`). For now she ties up at a rough jetty at the foot of Frostpine Ridge:
the fjord has frozen over this year, so you can see Icefang Hamlet's turf huts and the cracked ice bridge
from the deck but can't land there. The fjord thaws when the landing and hamlet are built (atlas Phase 4).

## Landscape, shore to summit
1. **Icefang Fjord** `[proposed, the stage's new first zone]`: the landing. A narrow fjord of black rock
   and blue ice, and a fishing hamlet of turf-roofed huts. A great **ice bridge** that Queen Glacia froze
   across the strait to the next island hangs in the background, cracked down the middle.
2. **Frostpine Ridge** (exists): snow forest on the climb. Frostlings, yeti cubs.
3. **Starfall Summit** (exists): a crater where the **Astral Wyrm** fell. Glassy ground and crystal golems
   grown from star-dust. The sky up here is full of stars that are visibly *in the wrong places*: some
   are smeared and some are clumped.

## People
**The fjord folk:** pale, frosted slimes who go half-solid in the cold and move slowly. They remember the
Queen as good. Proposed AI character: **Old Skaldi**, a lamplighter who keeps the hamlet's fire and the
old songs (which hold Tier 2 lore).

## Story role
Act II. The world is bigger and sadder than you knew. Glacia's frozen bridge is a small version of
Chronos's mistake: she held on so hard that it froze her people. Beating the Astral Wyrm puts the stars
back, so ships can steer again, which opens the voyage south.

## Change from today
The trail from the Wildwood is gone: Frostpeak is reached by the ferry. It's still left by a "Caldera
pass", which goes when the Steam Straits ship runs. Its entrance becomes the fjord landing (Phase 4).
