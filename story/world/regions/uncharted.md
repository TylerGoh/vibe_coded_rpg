# Uncharted: islands and places for later

None of these are canon. Each one is a ready-made slot for a future stage, side area or event. Add more
freely, following `../../expansion.md`.

## The Maelstrom (centre of the sea)
Where Pangelea's heart sank. A vast slow whirlpool with the Citadel circling above it. After the main
arc, restarting the Wheel calms it, and something rises out of it: the first sign of the next Gathering.
**Post-game hook.**

## The Sunken Reach (under the sea)
The drowned lowlands of Pangelea: Keeper roads, sunken towns and coral grown over clockwork. Underwater
movement (slimes float!) would be a new physics mode in `step()`. Holds the other Keepers' story.

## Lantern Fen (east)
A marsh island lit by drifting ghost lights. Slimes there talk to their dead through the lanterns. Quiet
and strange, a side story about letting go (the theme from another angle). A good mid-level side island
between Frostpeak and Emberdeep.

## Places the world implies but nobody has named yet
- An island the Drift has already carried so far that it's only a smudge on the horizon. Ships that went
  looking never came back. **The edge of the known world.**
- A **desert** island somewhere (no desert exists yet, and that's a landscape type missing from the game).
- A **floating wreck** of a Keeper skyship, drifting between the Citadel and the sea.
- Small islets: a lighthouse rock, a hermit's isle, a seabird colony. Cheap side rooms for events.
