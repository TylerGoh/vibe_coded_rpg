# Emberdeep (far south)

**Tone:** harsh, hot and industrial. Grief and anger. People here are blunt and suspicious of
outsiders. This is where players learn what really happened.

## Getting there
A long ship voyage south through the **Steam Straits**: warm green water, geysers, fog, and the smoke
column on the horizon. It needs the stars restored (the Astral Wyrm beaten).

## Landscape, shore to heart
1. **Black Sand Shore** `[proposed, new first zone]`: obsidian beaches, a harbour of rusted iron, and
   hot springs where slimes soak. The last soft spot before the hard country.
2. **Cinder Flats** (exists): ash plains and dead forges. Half-buried giant gear-teeth that were never
   delivered to the Tidewheel stick out of the ash.
3. **Forgehold** `[proposed]`: the forge-slimes' town, cut into the caldera wall. Bellows, chains and
   heat-haze. A memorial wall of smiths' names.
4. **Magma Heart** (exists): the volcano's core. **The Molten Forge**, where Ignis made the Tidewheel's
   gears, and **Phoenix Roost** at the rim.
5. **The thermal column** `[proposed]`: the volcano's updraft. The balloon to the Citadel launches here.

## People
**The forge-slimes:** dense, dark, ember-flecked slimes, thinned and hardened by the heat. They blame the
Keepers for the Stopped Hour, and they blame themselves for making the gears that jammed. Proposed AI
character: **Forgewarden Ash**, gruff, who'll only tell you the truth once you've beaten Ignis.

## Story role
Act III. The truth: the Tidewheel, the Keepers, the Stopped Hour, and that the Drift was never meant to
be forever. Pyra's broken cycle shows what's wrong at the deepest level. The volcano's heat literally
lifts you to the final act.
