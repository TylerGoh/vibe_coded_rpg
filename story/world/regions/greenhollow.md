# Greenhollow (home island): Slimeville and the Wildwood

**Tone:** cozy and storybook. Puns welcome. Danger is light, and fainting is a bump on the head.

## Landscape, west to east
1. **Greenhollow Harbour** (exists): piers, a stubby lighthouse, fishing boats, and the ferry Puddle
   Jumper. The lamp was dark for years (its keeper, Granny Wicks, moved inland) and Captain Brine wouldn't
   sail with a dark light behind him; then Granny Wicks came home and lit it, and the ferry runs again. It's at the village's west end, past the furniture store. You can
   see Frostpeak's aurora faintly to the north at dusk.
2. **Slimeville** (exists): the bay village round the old apple tree. Bakery, Inn, Merchant Pip, the
   Lecture Hall, the Music House, the Museum.
3. **The Wildwood** (exists, `FIELD`): the island's interior, rising east toward its spine.
   - *Sprout Meadow*: open grassland, the Sprout King's little "kingdom" of hopping sprouts.
   - *Mushroom Grove*: damp lowland where the mycelium net surfaces. Faint glowing threads run into
     the ground and, in the story, under the sea.
   - *Bramble Woods*: thorn thicket climbing to the ridge. Inside it are **Keeper ruins** (exist): mossy
     standing stones with gear-shaped carvings, the first clue that someone else lived here once. Four
     can be read: the Waystone, the Fallen Gate, the Roll of Keepers (one row, an hourglass, scratched
     out on purpose) and the Root Stone (a carved tree whose roots run off the stone).
4. **Greenhollow's east cliffs** (exist): the Wildwood ends at sea cliffs; the trail to the
   Highlands beside them goes when the ferry runs. From the top you see open water in every direction, with Frostpeak far north and smoke far
   south. *This is the moment the player realises how far everything is.*

## People
Slimeville's villagers (the real players), Elder Mochi, Curator Clio, Merchant Pip, the baker, the
innkeeper. **Captain Brine** (exists, AI), the old ferry captain who mutters about the sea "stretching"
(see `../../characters/people.md`).

## Story role
Act I. Home, the start of the mystery, and the place you come back to. As acts pass, Slimeville should
change *a little*: a lit lighthouse, sailors from other islands at the Inn, trade goods at Pip's.
