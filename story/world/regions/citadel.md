# The Aether Citadel (in the sky)

**Tone:** eerie, solemn and epic. Almost no living people. Wind, ticking, and echoes of the Keepers.
Grown-up writing with no whimsy at all, except Mochi's memories of the place, which are the only warmth
in it.

## Getting there
A balloon riding Emberdeep's thermal column up through layers of cloud. Below, the whole archipelago
shrinks into a scatter of specks on the Glass Sea. This is the best view in the game and the clearest
"look how far apart they are" moment.

## Landscape, dock to spire
1. **The Skydocks** `[proposed, new first zone]`: broken piers hanging off the city's underside, with
   empty mooring rings. Wind constantly.
2. **Cogwork Bastion** (exists): the Keepers' workshops and walls, still run by automatons that never
   got told to stop. Coglings, sentry drones.
3. **The Grand Foundry** (exists, the Colossus den): the Tidewheel's housing. The Wheel itself is huge,
   half-visible, jammed with one tooth snapped.
4. **Chrono Spire** (exists): the axle of the Wheel, rising into the sky. Time is wrong here: rain hangs
   mid-air and echoes of Keepers repeat their last moments. Chrono wraiths are what's left of them.
5. **The Hour Sanctum** (exists, Chronos's den): the top of the spire, a room stopped at the Stopped Hour.

## Story role
Act IV. The confrontation, and the choice. Restarting the Wheel means accepting the Drift. After that
the Citadel can become a peaceful, windswept place that friends visit for the view.
