# History of the world

Dates are deliberately vague ("ages", "generations") so later stories can slot in without arithmetic.

## The Breathing Ages (deep past)
- One land, **Pangelea**. It gathers together and drifts apart over ages, like a slow breath. Each
  Gathering is a golden age of everyone together, and each Drift is a long age of distance, which
  slimes have always known will end.
- The **Heartroot** runs under all the land and keeps it alive and joined.
- The **Tidewheel** sets the rhythm. The **Keepers** tend it from their seat at the centre (the
  Citadel). Each Keeper looks after one part of the Wheel. One is the **Keeper of Hours**.
- The forge-slimes of the south make the Wheel's gears. Each turn of the cycle is marked by the Ember
  Phoenix (Pyra) dying and being reborn.

## The last Gathering
- A long, happy age. The Keeper of Hours, **Pell**, has an apprentice: a young green slime called
  **Mochi**.
- As the Gathering's end nears, Pell can't accept that the Drift will scatter everyone he loves, perhaps
  for ever in his lifetime. He decides to stop the Wheel at the last hour of the Gathering.

## The Stopped Hour
- Pell jams the Wheel. It doesn't stop cleanly. A tooth snaps, and the Wheel locks in a half-turn that
  only pushes outward.
- Pangelea breaks. The centre sinks (the Maelstrom, the Sunken Reach). The Keepers' seat tears loose into
  the sky. The stars are knocked out of place.
- The Wheel's snapped teeth, the **Anchor shards**, scatter across the islands. Some sink into great
  creatures and twist them (the bosses). How many shards there are is unknown.
- Pell, inside the Citadel, becomes **Chronos**, time-sick and alone, still holding the broken hour.
- Mochi, thrown clear to Greenhollow, plants a cutting of the Heartroot there. It grows into the apple
  tree. Mochi has tended it ever since and stays old but alive near it. He has told no one.

## The Drift (now)
- Generations pass. The islands forget each other. Slimeville forgets the Keepers entirely.
- The drift is speeding up. Lately the beasts of the Wildwood have grown restless, because their shards
  are stirring as Chronos draws on them to finish stopping time.
- **The main arc starts here.**

## After the main arc `[proposed]`
- The Wheel turns again. The Drift becomes a normal Drift: slow, and someday returning.
- The routes stay open, and the sea becomes a place people cross. The next age's stories happen across
  water (see `../expansion.md`).
