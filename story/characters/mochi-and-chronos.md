# Elder Mochi and Chronos: the two halves of the story

They were teacher and apprentice. Both loved the same world and lost it in the same moment. One
froze, and one kept tending a tree. The whole arc is the space between them.

## Elder Mochi
- **Surface (Tier 0):** the kindly grandparent by the apple tree. Puns and a sleepy "hmm…".
- **Truth:** Pell's apprentice from the last Gathering. He planted the Heartroot cutting (the apple tree)
  and has lived impossibly long beside it. He knows about the Keepers, the Wheel and his old master. He
  has kept quiet out of shame (he didn't stop Pell) and kindness (why burden a happy village?).
- **How he changes:** he reveals one tier at a time as players return with proof (beaten bosses, shards).
  He never lies, but early on he deflects ("old slimes forget things, hmm…"). By Act IV his warmth is
  the only warmth in the Citadel's story, and he asks the players to bring Pell home, not to destroy him.
- **Voice rule:** always warm. What changes with the tier is how honest he is, not the tone of his voice.

## Chronos, Keeper of Hours (once Pell)
- **Want:** that nothing he loves ever leaves again. **Method:** stop time entirely and freeze every
  island where it stands. **Flaw:** he can't tell keeping from caging.
- He isn't cruel, and he's terribly tired. In the fight he speaks in fragments of the Stopped Hour. His
  ultimates are his philosophy: *Time Stop* (don't move, stay as you are) and *Collapse of Hours* (the
  walls closing in).
- **Resolution `[proposed]`:** beaten, he doesn't die. He's set free from the broken hour and becomes
  Pell again: an old, unsure slime. He could come down to Slimeville to sit by the apple tree with Mochi.
  This gives the end a quiet, grown-up note, and it's a new AI character for the post-game.

## Curator Clio
- Slimeville's historian. Her museum's seven wings hold real-world human history, and that stays,
  accurate and sober (`docs/places/museum.md`).
- **Bridge into the story `[proposed]`:** Clio is the first to take the Keeper ruins seriously. A new
  museum wing, **"The Slime Ages"**, grows as players bring back lore: rubbings of Keeper stones, a
  Tidewheel tooth, a star chart. In it Clio is a field historian of *this* world, with the same rules
  (never invent, say when it's uncertain), applied to canon.
- Where the human-history wings come from in-world is left open (`../open-questions.md`).
