# The bosses: creatures that carry Anchor shards

Every boss carries an **Anchor shard**, a snapped tooth of the Tidewheel, which twisted it. Each one is a
small story about the arc's theme: holding on versus letting go. Beating a boss **frees** the creature
(it respawns because the shard keeps pulling it back until the Wheel turns again: that's the in-world
reason for respawns). Players bring back proof (lore, a shard sliver) that opens the next tier of truth.

Order is by level. Names are the game's current ones.

## Greenhollow / the Wildwood (cozy; light stakes)
- **Sprout King** (Sprout King's Hollow). A sprout who got a shard and crowned himself. Bossy, small and
  funny. *Royal Decree* is a tantrum. Beaten, he's just a sprout again and a little relieved.
- **Mother Mushroom** (Mother Mushroom's Grotto). The mycelium network that still threads under the sea
  to other islands. Her shard gave her a fever, and the network screams instead of whispering. Beaten,
  she rests, and the network carries faint voices from far islands. This is the **first proof other
  islands exist**. Very sympathetic.
- **Bramble Behemoth** (The Briar Throne). The island's guardian. It grew the briar to wall in the Keeper
  ruins and to keep Greenhollow from drifting. Protective to the point of smothering. Beaten, it opens the
  ruins: the first carvings of the Wheel, and the end of Act I.

- **The Tidemother** (The Tidemother's Hoard, Saltspray Coves; shipped G1). A great hermit crab with a shard
  in her shell who keeps everything the sea washes in and won't let the tide take anything back out; the
  pools have gone stale. Her lines (MOBS.tidemother.lines) are a hoarder's panic. Freed, she lets the
  tide have it, and the water runs clean.
- **The Knotmole** (The Knotted Burrow, the Mycelium Deep; shipped G2). An old blind mole who dug down to
  the warm threads and, with a shard in her paw, tied them into knots to keep the warmth for herself, so
  the threads stopped carrying anything out to sea. Her lines (MOBS.knotmole.lines) are a cold, lonely
  creature's grip. Freed, she lets go, and the glow runs off under the sea again: a quiet hint that the
  net links the islands.
- **The Root Warden** (The Warden's Court, Keeper's Spine; shipped G5; Greenhollow's party den). A Keeper
  guardian of grey stone, set at the end of the high road to hold a sealed gate "until we come again". The
  Keepers never came. The roots (the ones that run under all the land, west toward the village and the
  apple tree, though it never says so) grew up through every joint, and the shard lodged in its back keeps
  it standing. It isn't cruel; it's a sentry who has forgotten every order but "hold". Its lines
  (MOBS.root_warden.lines) wear down to that one word. Freed, it understands they aren't coming back and
  lets the roots have it: the gate stays shut (what's behind it is not for Greenhollow to know), but the
  road is the players'. Its ultimate, Hold Fast, is roots bursting up everywhere but one lane. Tier 1 only:
  the Keepers, a gate, a guardian; nothing of the Wheel or the Heartroot by name.

## Frostpeak (melancholy)
- **Frost Queen Glacia** (The Frozen Throne). She froze the strait into an ice bridge to hold her island
  close to its neighbour, and she's frozen her own people half-solid doing it. The clearest small mirror
  of Chronos. Beaten, she lets the bridge go, and that's sad but right.
- **Astral Wyrm** (Starfall Sanctum). The star-serpent sailors once steered by. It was knocked out of the
  sky in the Stopped Hour and crashed into the summit, and the stars went wrong with it. Beaten, it rises
  back to the sky, the stars return, and ships can navigate the long voyage south.
- **Sigrun, the Queen's Shield** (The Seam, at the crack in the Queen's Bridge; Frostpeak's party den, shipped
  F4). Glacia's sworn guard and the captain of her guard, fjord folk frozen half to ice. Glacia froze the
  strait to hold on; Sigrun swore to hold what her queen held, and goes on freezing the crack shut every night
  though her queen has let go: the harder Glacia story, holding on to someone else's holding on. Her guards
  stand frozen at their posts along the bridge (some were hamlet folk). Her lines (MOBS.sigrun.lines) are an
  oath nobody released her from; freed, she stops mending it ("Cross while it lasts"). The shard is set in her
  shield's boss. Skaldi knew her as a girl. Tier 2 only by showing.
- **The Old Narwhal** (The Breathing Hole, the Whale Skerries; shipped F2; the atlas's "sea creature under the
  ice bridge"). The last of her pod. When the islands drifted and the sea grew wide, the pod swam south along
  the old whale road and she stayed; with a shard lodged at the root of her tusk she holds the floes shut
  across the outer sound so nothing else can ever leave the fjord: not the fish, not the seals. Things may
  come in (the ferry does); nothing goes out. The fish are penned in and thin. A small mirror of Glacia's
  holding on, from below. Her lines (MOBS.old_narwhal.lines) are loneliness turned to a grip. Freed, she sings
  them out past the skerries, the way she should have sung the pod. The Narwhal Grotto, beside her den, is
  where the pod shed their tusks; one very small one lies apart (her calf's, though nothing says so).
  Tier 2 only by showing.
- **The Pale Wyrm** (a field boss in the Aurora Heights' Wyrmfall; shipped F3). Something of the Astral Wyrm's
  kind, a small dim star-serpent that fell with it when the stars fell. It lies curled in a hollow it melted
  into the snow, where the aurora comes down. It could rise; it would rather stay fallen: up there it was
  steered by, and when it fell the boats were lost; down here nobody looks to it. The harder Wyrm story:
  holding on to being fallen. Struck, it lifts and falls again and again (its `dive`), eyes shut unless
  striking. As a field boss it has no lines: the Serpent Stone (one small star near the tail cut deeper),
  Skaldi's one-line song ("It lies in the snow, where nobody steers by it.") and Eyvind ("tired") tell it.
  Whether it carries a shard is left open. Tier 2 only by showing.

## Emberdeep (harsh)
- **Ignis the Magma Tyrant** (The Molten Forge). The master smith who forged the Tidewheel's gears. He
  has been consumed by guilt that his tooth snapped, so he forges and smashes gears endlessly. The anger
  is grief. Beaten, he gives the hard truth (Tier 3) in his last coherent words.
- **Pyra the Ember Phoenix** (Phoenix Roost). The bird of the cycle. She dies and is reborn at every turn
  of the Wheel, but the Wheel doesn't turn, so she is stuck burning at the threshold. Her *Rebirth*
  ultimate is a broken, desperate attempt to finish the cycle. Beaten, she shows players the way up the
  thermal column, because only the Wheel can set her free.
- **The Hatchkeeper** (The Buried Clutch, the Smoking Coast; shipped E2). A great black marine iguana, older
  than anyone. Her first clutch hatched long ago and ran down to the sea, every one, and the sea had grown wide
  and did not give them back. With a shard lodged under her crest she keeps every clutch since buried in sand
  the lava keeps hot, warm forever, so it never hatches and never leaves her. Holding on to her young: Eyvind and
  Liv's story told from the other side (Liv sits up with the nest some nights, out of reach, and knows it). Her
  lines (MOBS.hatchkeeper.lines) are a mother's hush turned to a grip. Ultimate from the landscape: Boiling Sand,
  the whole beach boiling but the tide-wet patches. Freed, she hears them tapping and lets them go: the eggs hatch
  and the little ones run down to the sea. She buries a new clutch each time she comes back (only Liv says so).
  Tier 0 only; nothing in her den shows the Wheel.

## The Aether Citadel (solemn)
- **The Clockwork Colossus** (The Grand Foundry). The Wheel's guardian body, still following its last
  order: *protect the Keeper*. It has no malice, only obedience. *Overclock* is the Wheel straining.
- **Chronos, Keeper of Hours** (The Hour Sanctum). See `mochi-and-chronos.md`.

## Future bosses
More shards exist than these nine. A new boss is a new creature twisted by a shard, plus a small story
about holding on or letting go, fitted to its island's climate and tone (`../expansion.md`).
